# Trademark Policy — Maho

*(DRAFT — 결정 대기 항목: §"Policy strength"의 A/B/C 중 하나를 선택해야 확정됩니다.)*

The Sustainable Use License grants you rights to the **software**. It does **not**
grant any rights to the **Maho name, logo, or other brand assets**. This document
states how those marks may and may not be used.

## Marks covered

- The word mark **"Maho"** and the product name **"Maho Browser"**
- The Maho logo, app icon, and installer artwork (`maho-chromium/branding/**`)
- The domain names and official distribution channels

## Policy strength

Pick one before publication. The draft below states option **B**, the recommended
middle setting.

| Option | Rule | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Permissive** | Forks may keep the name if they add a clear "unmodified/modified by" notice | Maximizes adoption, weakens provenance |
| **B — Rebrand required (recommended)** | Forks must remove the name and logo and use their own branding; factual, non-prominent references to the upstream project are allowed | Preserves provenance, standard practice for source-available projects |
| **C — Strict** | No use of the name at all, including "based on Maho" phrasing without written permission | Cleanest legally, most friction for the community |

## Rules under option B

**Allowed without permission**

- Referring to the project factually: "based on Maho", "a fork of Maho", "compatible with Maho"
- Linking to the official project or its release pages
- Using the name in academic papers, blog posts, reviews, and news coverage
- Redistributing unmodified official binaries, provided you do not imply endorsement

**Not allowed without written permission**

- Using "Maho" or the Maho logo in a product name, app name, package name, domain, or social handle
- Shipping a modified build under the Maho name or logo
- Selling, hosting, or supporting a modified build as "Maho" or implying it is official
- Using the marks in a way that suggests sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation

## Modified builds

If you distribute a modified build:

1. Remove the Maho name and logo and use your own branding.
2. Do not use the official app icon or installer artwork.
3. Add a prominent notice stating that the software has been modified, as required by the Sustainable Use License ("Notices").
4. Keep all copyright and license notices intact.

## Official binaries

Only binaries distributed through Maho's official channels are "official builds".
Nothing in the source license makes a third-party build official. Builds produced
by third parties carry no warranty, support, update, or security-patch commitment
from Maho.

## Reporting misuse

Report trademark misuse to `admin@mahobrowser.com`. We prefer to resolve
situations informally first. If you are unsure whether a use is permitted, ask
before shipping.
