# Demo 01: Zero-Setup AI

**Duration:** ~55 seconds
**Premise:** A brand-new user signs up, opens the AI panel, asks a question, and gets a real answer. No API keys. No model picker. No configuration wizard.
**Primary message:** Other AI browsers want a credit card and three forms before you can ask a single question. Maho doesn't.

---

## Act 1: Hook (0:00 - 0:05)

**On-screen action**
- Cold open on the Maho welcome screen. Cursor hovers over "Sign in".
- Lower-third caption: "Most AI browsers want your API key. Maho doesn't."

**Voiceover**
> "You just downloaded a browser. You want to use AI. Watch how fast that actually is."

---

## Act 2: Value (0:05 - 0:45)

**Beat 1: Sign up (0:05 - 0:12)**
- Type email into the signup form. Click continue.
- Cut to inbox. Click the magic-link button. Tab returns to the browser, signed in.

> "Email. Magic link. You're in. No password, no plan picker."

**Beat 2: Open the AI panel (0:12 - 0:22)**
- Mouse moves to the sidebar. Highlight the Lucide sparkles icon. Click it.
- AI panel slides open from the right. Empty thread, ready for input.
- **Important on-screen check:** no API-key dialog appears. No "Choose your model" modal. Nothing.

> "Click the AI button. No setup wizard. No API key prompt. No model picker. The panel just opens."

**Beat 3: Ask a real question (0:22 - 0:35)**
- Navigate to a long article. A Wikipedia page on the Apollo program works well for the visual length.
- Click the prompt box. Type: "Summarize this page in five bullet points."
- Press enter.

> "Open any page. Ask anything. Here, you want a five-bullet summary."

**Beat 4: Streaming response (0:35 - 0:45)**
- Response streams in token by token. Visible cursor blink. Five clean bullets.
- Zoom in on the response. No "configure your provider" error. No empty state.

> "Real model. Real tokens. You didn't paste a single key."

---

## Act 3: CTA (0:45 - 0:55)

**On-screen action**
- Cut to a card on the Maho hero color. Text: "Every account: $0.50 of AI every month, free."
- Below that: "Need more? Pro is $5/mo." Tiny print: "Bring your own key any time, no markup."
- End card with the Maho logo and the URL.

**Voiceover**
> "Every Maho account gets fifty cents of AI a month, on us. Hit the ceiling and Pro is five a month. Or bring your own key and pay nothing. Download Maho."

---

## Production notes

- Record at 1920x1080, 60fps. Crop to 1080x1080 for the social cut.
- Single take per beat, no jump cuts inside a beat.
- Mouse cursor at 1.5x scale for visibility on mobile.
- No music under the first sentence. Music in on Beat 1, soft, ends on the CTA card.
- The streaming response is the hero shot. Don't trim it.
