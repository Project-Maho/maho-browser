# Demo 02: Tab Tidy

**Duration:** ~50 seconds
**Premise:** You've been researching all afternoon. Twenty-five tabs across three topics. One click and Maho groups them into named folders.
**Primary message:** Your tab strip looks like a kitchen drawer. Maho cleans it up in one click.

---

## Act 1: Hook (0:00 - 0:05)

**On-screen action**
- Open on a wide shot of the Maho sidebar with 25 tabs stacked vertically. Titles overlap. Favicons blur together.
- Mouse scrolls down the tab list. The list keeps scrolling. And scrolling.
- Lower-third caption: "25 tabs. Three topics. Zero structure."

**Voiceover**
> "You spent the afternoon researching. Now your tab strip looks like this. Sound familiar?"

---

## Act 2: Value (0:05 - 0:45)

**Beat 1: Show the mess (0:05 - 0:12)**
- Hover over a few tab titles. Tooltip reveals topics: SwiftUI animation, sourdough hydration, Rust async, sourdough starter, watercolor brushes, SwiftUI gestures.
- Zoom out. The mess is the point.

> "Three projects bleeding into each other. You can't find anything."

**Beat 2: Find the button (0:12 - 0:20)**
- Mouse moves to the top of the sidebar. Highlight the Lucide wand-2 icon.
- Tooltip reads "Tidy tabs".
- Click it.

> "One button at the top of the sidebar. Wand icon. Click it."

**Beat 3: The suggestion (0:20 - 0:35)**
- Modal slides in. Header: "Maho suggests three folders."
- Folder cards listed:
  - **SwiftUI work** (9 tabs)
  - **Sourdough** (8 tabs)
  - **Rust async deep-dive** (8 tabs)
- Each card expands to show the tab titles inside. User can rename a folder or drag a tab between folders before applying.

> "You see three suggested folders. You can rename them. You can move a tab. Nothing happens until you say so."

**Beat 4: Apply (0:35 - 0:45)**
- Click "Apply".
- Tab strip animates. Tabs slide into their folders. Sidebar collapses to three named groups with a tab count.
- Click a folder. It expands. Click again. It collapses.

> "Hit apply. Your sidebar collapses to three folders. Click to expand, click to collapse."

---

## Act 3: CTA (0:45 - 0:50)

**On-screen action**
- End card on the Maho hero color. Text: "Tidy 25 tabs in one click."
- Subtext: "Built into every Maho install."
- Maho logo and the URL.

**Voiceover**
> "Twenty-five tabs. One click. Free in every Maho install. Download Maho."

---

## Production notes

- Use a real research session. Stage 25 tabs across exactly three topics so the AI grouping looks crisp on camera.
- Capture the wand-2 button at 1.5x cursor scale. It's the visual anchor of the whole demo.
- The folder suggestion modal is the second hero shot. Hold it for at least three full seconds.
- Animate the tab collapse. Don't cut to it. The motion is what sells the feature.
- Soft synth music throughout. Drop to silence on the click of "Apply" so the animation reads.
