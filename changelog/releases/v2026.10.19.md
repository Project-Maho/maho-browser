Maho 2026.10.19 brings WebAuthn passkeys, password and OTP autofill, and automatic device-key unlock to Maho Vault, while isolating built-in agent tools inside OS-native sandboxes on macOS, Linux, and Windows. It also adds native document artifact creation (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX), Ask Maho from page selections and area captures, local multilingual episodic memory, controller pairing for external CLI/MCP clients, and cosmetic ad-blocking diagnostics.
