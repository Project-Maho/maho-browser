Maho 2026.9.24 focuses on making automated browsing trustworthy: agents now perceive the page through a native visual-object model instead of chasing snapshots, can ask a human for help instead of stalling, borrow a tab and hand it back, and record their work as a repeatable routine. Alongside that, Memory Saver and Tab Sleep give long sessions a smaller footprint, and the favorites grid reaches Arc parity for tile customization.
