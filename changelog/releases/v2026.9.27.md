Maho 2026.9.27 makes Maho Mail dependable with Google: calendars sync with the calendar.events permission alone, events save from the unified view, message bodies render in the reader, and Gmail special folders are recognized. It also adds account-escrowed Vault unlock across devices, brings Ctrl+L address entry in line with Arc, and fixes favorites, the sidebar, and several macOS stability issues.
