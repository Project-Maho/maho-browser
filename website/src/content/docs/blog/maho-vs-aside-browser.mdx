## Where Aside leads, and why benchmarks are the wrong measure

Aside ranks #1 on three browser agent benchmarks. We're not going to pretend otherwise. If your mental model is "AI does work for me while I'm not looking," Aside leads.

**We think that's the wrong mental model.**

Autonomous agent products in 2025–26 share a documented failure surface, not specific to Aside, but inherent to the paradigm Aside leads. The category has produced agents that bought the wrong flight, replied to a boss with hallucinated context, and leaked credentials to a phishing page that looked official. Benchmarks measure task completion under sanitized conditions. They don't measure what happens when something goes wrong in your actual life.

Maho's answer isn't a worse agent. It's a different question: what would AI look like if it ran when you asked, on data you saw, with consent every step? That's a different product. The rest of this page is about that product.

## Where Maho wins

### Cross-platform from day one

Aside is macOS only. Maho ships macOS today, with iPhone and Android in development. Spaces, bookmarks, and history sync end-to-end encrypted across every device you own. If you work across phone and laptop, Aside makes you choose. Maho doesn't.

### Free forever, no credit meter

Aside's free tier gives you 500 credits per month. Heavy users hit the wall. Maho's free tier gives you unlimited BYOK, you bring your own API key (OpenAI, Anthropic, or local Ollama) and pay your provider directly. No middleman markup, no credit anxiety. If you'd rather not manage keys, Maho Pro is $5/mo and Max is $20/mo, flat fees with clear model ceilings, not metered.

### Steered AI, not autonomous AI

Think of it like driving. Aside is full-self-driving: the agent picks the route, navigates the websites, taps the buttons. Maho is hand-on-wheel: you steer, the AI accelerates. Summarize this page. Clean up these tab titles. Organize my downloads. Draft a reply, but let me send it. The AI is powerful, it just doesn't act in your accounts when you're not watching. For most real work, that's not a limitation. It's the point.

### Spaces, not tabs

Aside organizes your browser around AI tasks. Maho organizes around projects. Each Space is a persistent workspace with its own tabs, AI thread, notes, and split view state. Switch contexts in one click. Pick up tomorrow exactly where you left off.

### Sync that's actually private

Both browsers claim local-first. Maho's sync architecture is documented publicly: X25519 handshake, six-digit verification code, AES-GCM per-message authentication. The full design lives in our engineering blog at [/blog/sync-architecture-end-to-end](/blog/sync-architecture-end-to-end). Aside doesn't publish their sync design.

## Which one is right for you?

**Pick Aside if** you primarily work in a few logged-in tools, your work is repetitive enough that an agent can finish multi-step tasks, you're macOS-only, and you're comfortable letting AI take actions in your accounts.

**Pick Maho if** you work across multiple devices (or plan to), you organize work by project rather than by task, you prefer AI that runs only when you ask, and you'd rather pay $5–$20/mo with PAYG credits than $20–$200/mo for managed agentic AI.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Maho a fork of Aside?

No. Both are independent Chromium forks.

### Why doesn't Maho compete on agent benchmarks?

Different product philosophy. We chose Spaces + on-demand AI over autonomous agents. See our reasoning on the [/why](/why) page.

### Can I migrate from Aside to Maho?

Yes. Maho imports bookmarks, history, and passwords from Aside and most Chromium-based browsers on first launch.

### Is Maho open source like Brave or Firefox?

No. Maho is proprietary, the same as Aside. We focus instead on external verification methods, public architecture documentation, and direct contact with the developer.
